  • Compute geometry disabled until pano exists; rail editor disabled until geometry exists.
  • FLOOR and SIDE sit side by side with gizmos visible; origin star at (0,0,0) and not draggable.
  • Preview flight sits under both plates. Mesh-only flight preview plays before Confirm (black holes expected).
  • Switching chips restores generated views (Panorama ERP, Geometry preview + Confirm).
  • colmap/sparse/0 exists after Reconstruct. Viewport shows the sparse cloud, not cube-face stills.
  • Background jobs update the strip under the chips (not log-only).
  • If gsplat passed: splat.ply with the selected strategy (Splat3 default). Continue stays on that strategy; Splat3 ↔ MCMC needs Retrain. If failed: UI says so; COLMAP still valid for Brush.
  • Splat viewport is a live raster (not a point cloud). Reset is the rails Star. Max steps is any integer ≥ 1. Stop kills training.
  • MCMC uses the same per-Gaussian raster as Splat3. Needle takeover / glowing whites / crushed blacks are bugs.
